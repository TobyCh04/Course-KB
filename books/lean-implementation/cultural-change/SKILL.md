# Cultural Change Fundamentals (文化变革基础)

## R — Reading

> "Any time any major culture-changing initiative is undertaken, such as the implementation of a Lean initiative, three fundamental issues must be addressed: Do we have the leadership to make this a success? Do we have the motivation to make this a success? Do we have the necessary problem solvers in place to make this a success?" — Ch 6, p.87

## I — Interpretation

Culture must change BEFORE Lean tools are applied. Three fundamental issues:

**1. Leadership (领导力)**
- A leader is someone who has a following — people willing to do what he says because HE says it
- Critical characteristics: competence, character, personality, position
- The Lean leader must be willing to do the work, not delegate everything

**2. Motivation (动力)**
- Why are we doing this? What's in it for the workers?
- Fear of job loss is NOT a sustainable motivator
- Intrinsic motivation: empowerment, problem-solving authority, career growth

**3. Problem Solving (问题解决)**
- Everyone must be a problem solver, not just engineers
- In TPS, problems are seen as system weakness, not personal failure
- Workers can stop the line — this is revolutionary
- Time-based escalation: workers solve simple problems, escalate on a trigger

**Additional cultural concepts:**
- Interdependence: Lean requires teamwork across functions
- Foundational issues: 5S, standard work must be in place before flow
- Jidoka concept: quality problems are opportunities, not failures

## A1 — Past Application

The ABC Widgets case (Ch 17) failed because culture wasn't addressed. After 4 years of Lean initiative, basic problems persisted. The company wanted to LOOK Lean rather than BE Lean. Internal conflicts and insufficient onsite resources created massive resistance.

## A2 — Future Trigger

- "We tried Lean before but it didn't stick"
- "Workers are resisting the Lean initiative"
- "Is our management committed enough for Lean?"
- "How do we get buy-in for Lean?"
- "Who should lead our Lean initiative?"

## E — Execution

1. **Assess leadership**: Run the Five Tests of Management Commitment (Ch 19)
2. **Assess motivation**: Why are we doing this? What's the benefit for workers?
3. **Assess problem solving**: Can workers solve problems? Do they have authority?
4. **Address gaps**: Train leaders, create motivation, build problem-solving skills
5. **THEN apply tools**: Only after cultural foundation is solid
6. **Monitor**: Culture is ongoing — continuously reinforce

## B — Boundary

- **NOT for**: Quick-fix situations (culture change takes time)
- **NOT for**: Organizations where management won't participate
- **Requires**: Genuine management commitment (not just lip service)
- **Warning**: If culture isn't addressed, tools will be applied but gains won't sustain
- **Limitation**: The book acknowledges cultural discussion is incomplete — it's a starting point, not comprehensive
