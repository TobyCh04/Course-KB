# House of Lean (精益屋)

## R — Reading

> "The House of Lean is a metaphor that is designed to show how the various topics discussed in Lean manufacturing fit together and interact." — Ch 20, p.299

## I — Interpretation

A visual framework showing the complete Lean system:

**Roof (目标): Objectives**
- Better — Best quality
- Faster — Reduced lead times
- Cheaper — At lower cost
- Through the total elimination of waste

**Left Pillar (支柱): JIT**
- Takt, Balanced operations, Pull, Minimum lot size, Flow
- Lead time reductions, Leveling, Kanban, Cells, SMED/OTS
- Stores/buffer/safety stocks

**Right Pillar (支柱): Jidoka**
- Poka-yoke, 5 Whys, Kaizen, CIP

**Foundation (基础): Stability + People**
- Stability: Standard Work, Transparency, 5S, TPM, Process simplification, Sustaining the gains
- People: Multiskilled workers, Problem solving by all, Understanding of variation, OEE, MSA, Cp/Cpk, Availability, C/T reductions

**Key insight**: The two pillars are strategies (JIT + Jidoka), tactics are small-group activities (kanban, cells), and skills are individual behaviors (sizing kanban, making kanban).

## A1 — Past Application

The author developed a House of Lean for a specific client based on their individual needs. It served as a teaching tool and communication vehicle for the entire Lean initiative. The client found it made the overall effort more easily visualized and understood.

## E — Execution

1. **Assess current state**: Which elements of the House are already in place?
2. **Identify gaps**: What's missing? (Usually: stability foundation is weak)
3. **Prioritize**: Foundation first (5S, standard work, TPM), then pillars (JIT, Jidoka), then objectives
4. **Create your own House**: Customize to your facility's specific needs
5. **Communicate**: Use the House to teach all employees the Lean vision
6. **Track progress**: Check off elements as they're implemented

## B — Boundary

- **NOT for**: Specific tactical guidance (use individual tools instead)
- **NOT for**: Non-manufacturing contexts without adaptation
- **Limitation**: The House is a teaching/communication tool, not an implementation methodology
- **Note**: Each facility should create its own House based on specific needs
